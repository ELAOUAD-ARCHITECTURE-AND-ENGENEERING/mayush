**Date:** 2026-04-24
**Project:** Mayush (https://mayushdesign.com)
**Project Nature:** Multi-seller Marketplace for Interior Design & Home Products.

## 2. Competitive Marketplace Landscape
The competitive set includes general retailers and specialized home e-shops:
- **Guzel Home** (guzelhome.ma): Strong on Turkish furniture and kit-based products.
- **Kitea / IKEA Morocco**: Large-scale direct competitors (single-brand but high search volume).
- **Jumia Home**: General marketplace competitor with a strong furniture section.
- **Decoraa.ma / Homedeco.ma**: Specialized niche players in the home decor segment.

## 4. International Keyword Conflicts & GEO Nuances
- **"Marketplace"**: Users often confuse "Marketplace" with "Facebook Marketplace". Mayush must optimize for "Marketplace Meubles" to capture specific intent.
- **"Artisanal"**: High search volume in Morocco for "Artisanat Marocain". Mayush can win by positioning itself as the marketplace for local artisans to sell modern-traditional pieces.
- **"Livraison"**: The term "Livraison partout au Maroc" is a critical long-tail modifier that drives high conversion in the marketplace sector.

## 5. Strategic Recommendations for Marketplace SEO
1. **Category Page Dominance**: Instead of individual products, focus on ranking Category pages (e.g., "Tables basses", "Canapés") using the marketplace's variety as a strength.
2. **Seller Entity SEO**: Allow sellers to have their own "storefront" pages optimized for their brand names (e.g., "Boutique [Seller Name] Mayush").
3. **Product Aggregation Schema**: Use AggregateOffer schema to show price ranges across multiple sellers for the same product type, which is highly effective for AI search engine comparisons.
4. **GEO Nuances**: Use "Casablanca" and "Rabat" as primary geo-modifiers in meta titles to capture the highest purchasing power regions.
